# Narrative bible (cosmology lives here; the game only ever shows observations, interpretations and contradictions)

> **Canon note.** *Stotkal* is the creator's artistic identity and established aesthetic. The Palimpsest, the Returned, the
> Quieting, the Witness, the four societies and every site below are **new game fiction inspired by that aesthetic**.
> Nothing here depicts, continues or imitates any specific existing artwork or artist's signature.

## The register
Intimate, contemplative, beautiful, slightly unsettling. Scale, material and light do the strangeness: a summer garden beside an
unknowable machine, an abandoned chrome tower under a pale sky. Citizens have relationships and preferences, so loss is specific
(a name, a lullaby nobody finished, a suitcase of toys shared by forty people) rather than abstract philosophy.
Avoid: neon cyberpunk, permanent darkness, cartoon robots, cluttered holographic UI, gratuitous horror.

## The Palimpsest
A world of overlapping layers of civilisations whose histories do not agree. The **Returned** are chrome-bodied people who awaken with
language, emotional instincts and incomplete personal memories. They are embodied: they need food, maintenance, shelter and each other.
The **creators are absent**. Their structures are still active in ways nobody fully understands; a tower may be a library, an organism, a
climate machine, or several at once. Different societies hold incompatible but partly useful explanations.

## The Quieting (what is observable; what is not)
**Observable:** memories lose their associations; infrastructure drifts out of calibration; some regions become hard to inhabit; powered
structures dim; a conduit severs; a monument's contents stop moving.
**Interpretations in play:** a tide; a debt; the machines resting; the creators forgetting; the cycle correcting itself.
**Never revealed in the first run:** the cause. It is *not* confirmed to be a simple simulation reset. Resonance (pressure from active
infrastructure) correlates with severity, which is why "Break the Recurrence" is plausible, and the Meridian Spire's purpose stays unclear.
Mechanical costs and forecasts are always exact even when the lore is not.

## The Witness
An embodied founding figure whose damaged continuity crosses cycles. The Witness recognises some remnants without full knowledge (which is why
records persist between runs and why legacies exist). Politically accountable to living citizens, not a deity. Part of the Witness's own history is
lost at the end of every cycle, whatever else is saved. Council dreams and ledgers return fragments of it (named fragments in category *witness*).

## The Monolith of Memory
An obelisk containing suspended flowers, hands, toys and city fragments. A landmark, an archive, possibly part of an ending, but never the one required answer:
*Preserve Testimony* (a record), *Reveal Older Infrastructure* (the buried network), *Anchor Continuity* (hold a place steady). A Monolith-less run still has all four routes.

## The societies (understandable philosophies; none canonically right)
* **The Glass Conservatory** preserves distinct lives and authentic records. Archives, protected gardens. Fears irreversible erasure; objects to forced merging.
  Cities: glass chambers, botanical interiors, solitary chrome figures. *Its cost:* preservation consumes attention and resources, and can curdle into hoarding.
* **The Common Signal** shares consciousness across connected settlements. Values network access and collective continuity; fears fragmentation; sees isolation as dangerous.
  Cities: precise cables, synchronised light, distributed structures. *Its cost:* consent becomes an administrative detail unless somebody insists it is not.
* **The Merciful Veil** removes selected painful memories to keep peace. Values local Coherence and controlled restoration; fears inherited conflict; thinks complete preservation can be cruel.
  Cities: mirrored courtyards, quiet chambers, filtered light. *Its cost:* who chooses what is painful?

## Endings (separate mechanical success from emotional cost)
* *Embodied Continuity*: shelters hold; the Witness lost part of her own history making room for others.
* *Shared Continuity*: the network holds; some who stayed apart are remembered only as gaps in it.
* *The Unbroken Record*: the record is whole, and for a while it was more tended than the people who made it.
* *Break the Recurrence*: the pattern is interrupted, and the world, relieved, forgets there was ever a pattern.
* Failure: it did not hold; what survived did so by smaller kindnesses than the plan.
Successful endings never imply that victory was erased immediately afterwards: the Chronicle names the surviving communities, the promises kept or broken, the sacrifices and the preserved fragments, then offers a legacy that may become a named ruin, an institution echo, or a restored character in another cycle.

## Reference run (a story systems should be able to tell)
Awaken beside a garden and a glass ridge; survey reveals the Choir Engine and an independent settlement holding a conduit; release the voices, gain population, accept the integration problem; a Sanctuary stabilises the new community, delaying a Foundry; decline the Signal's collective, negotiate passage with the Conservatory; become a federation of embodied communities; at turn 18 the forecast exposes the conduit: build an inland connection, reinforce it, or renegotiate; end in protected settlements, with some memories lost, including part of the Witness's own.
This is not scripted: every element exists as a system (sites, interpretations, requests, treaties, protection bands, federation obligations, Chronicle).
